# Find4Sport operational risk register

Review this register at each release gate and after a material incident or provider change. The service owner assigns named owners and due dates; role names below are accountable functions, not proof of assignment. Record decisions and evidence in the linked release/incident record. Risk scoring is qualitative until the service owner approves likelihood and impact scales.

## Cloud shared-responsibility checkpoints (ISO/IEC 27017)

| Control area | Find4Sport responsibility | Provider evidence / operator check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and access | Least privilege for Supabase, Vercel and Stripe operators; protect CI secrets; remove stale access. | Review provider access lists and MFA/SSO controls; retain dated review evidence. |
| Application and data | Enforce authorization/RLS, secret handling, data minimization, backups and safe migrations. | Supabase RLS/security review, backup/PITR configuration and restore evidence. |
| Deployment and network | Review production domains, deployment protection, environment separation and rollback compatibility. | Confirm Vercel project state, active SHA, domains and environment settings. |
| Payment processing | Keep payment decisions server-side; validate signed/idempotent webhook handling and ledger reconciliation. | Stripe Test Mode evidence and event-delivery/reconciliation records. |
| Monitoring and incident response | Define alert thresholds, owners, escalation routes and evidence retention. | Provider logs, alert routing, incident records and exercise outcomes. |

This register records engineering and operational risks; it is not a certification, legal assessment, or proof that provider settings have been applied. Update each status only when the specified evidence is attached and reviewed.
